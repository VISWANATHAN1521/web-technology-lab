Welcome to the Club

The Campus Sports Club gives every student a chance to play, train and compete. We conduct coaching sessions every evening and inter-department tournaments every semester.

See Our Games

Cricket

Net practice on Monday and Wednesday.

Football

Training on the main ground every Tuesday.

Badminton

Indoor courts open from 5 PM to 8 PM.

Athletics

Track events and fitness drills on Friday.

Why Join?

Sports build team spirit, discipline and good health. Members get coaching from trained staff and can represent the college in zonal and national level competitions.

Membership

Membership is free for all students. Fill the form at the sports office with your register number and the game you would like to play.

Rules

  • Wear proper sports kit.
  • Be on time for practice.
  • Respect the coach and players.
